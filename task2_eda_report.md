# Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis and Business Intelligence

## Objective

The objective of this task was to explore the cleaned e-commerce dataset, identify business patterns, answer key business questions with SQL, and create a static dashboard.

## Dataset Summary

- Total orders: 1,499
- Total revenue: INR 5,845,247.70
- Average order value: INR 3,899.43
- Delivered orders: 1,060
- Return rate: 15.08%

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Smart Watch was the top product by revenue.
- The South region generated the highest revenue, at approximately INR 1.94 million.
- Paid Search was the highest-performing acquisition channel, generating approximately INR 1.06 million in revenue.
- Fashion had the highest return rate at 16.58%.
- Monthly revenue reached its highest level in January 2025, at approximately INR 315,782.
- Customer CUS-0166 generated the highest lifetime revenue among repeat customers.

## SQL Business Questions Answered

1. What is the monthly revenue trend?
2. Which five products generate the most revenue?
3. Which region generates the highest revenue?
4. Which acquisition channel generates the highest revenue and order count?
5. Which product category has the highest return rate?
6. Which customers are the most valuable repeat customers?

## Dashboard

The static dashboard includes:

- Total orders
- Total revenue
- Average order value
- Return rate
- Monthly revenue trend
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by region
- Revenue by acquisition channel

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing investment on Paid Search, as it produces the highest revenue.
- Prioritize Electronics and Smart Watch inventory because they are the leading revenue drivers.
- Investigate Fashion returns to identify possible product-quality, sizing, or customer-expectation issues.
- Explore the South region further to understand why it outperforms the other regions.
- Create loyalty offers for high-value repeat customers.
